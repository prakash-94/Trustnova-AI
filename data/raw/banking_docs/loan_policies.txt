Citizens Bank — Loan Policies & Guidelines

1. PERSONAL LOANS

1.1 Eligibility Requirements
- Minimum age: 18 years
- Must be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident
- Minimum credit score: 620 (higher scores receive better rates)
- Minimum annual income: $24,000
- Debt-to-income ratio must not exceed 40%
- Must have an active Citizens Bank deposit account or be willing to open one

1.2 Loan Amounts and Terms
- Personal loan amounts range from $5,000 to $50,000
- Repayment terms: 36, 48, 60, or 84 months
- Fixed APR ranges from 7.49% to 22.99% based on creditworthiness
- No origination fees, no prepayment penalties
- Auto-pay discount: 0.25% APR reduction when enrolled in automatic payments

1.3 Application Process
- Apply online, by phone (1-800-340-5626), or in-branch
- Decision typically within 1-2 business days
- Funds disbursed within 3-5 business days after approval
- Co-signers allowed for applicants who do not meet credit requirements independently

2. HOME EQUITY LINES OF CREDIT (HELOC)

2.1 Overview
A Home Equity Line of Credit allows homeowners to borrow against the equity in their home. This is a revolving line of credit with a variable interest rate.

2.2 Requirements
- Minimum credit score: 680
- Maximum combined loan-to-value (CLTV): 80%
- Property must be a primary residence or secondary home
- Property appraisal required (bank-ordered, cost may be waived for qualified borrowers)

2.3 Terms
- Draw period: 10 years (interest-only payments allowed)
- Repayment period: 20 years (principal + interest payments)
- Credit line amounts: $17,500 to $500,000
- Variable APR based on Prime Rate + margin (currently Prime + 0.50% to Prime + 7.00%)
- Annual fee: $50 (waived for the first year)

3. AUTO LOANS

3.1 New Vehicle Financing
- APR from 5.49% for 36-month terms (rates as of 2024)
- Loan amounts up to $150,000
- Terms: 24 to 84 months
- Financing available for vehicles up to 1 model year old with fewer than 7,500 miles

3.2 Used Vehicle Financing
- APR from 5.99% for 36-month terms
- Vehicles must be no more than 7 model years old with fewer than 100,000 miles
- Minimum loan amount: $7,500

3.3 Refinancing
- Refinance existing auto loans from other lenders
- No application fee
- Rate discount of 0.25% with auto-pay enrollment

4. STUDENT LOANS

4.1 Private Student Loans
- For undergraduate and graduate students
- Variable APR from 4.99% or Fixed APR from 6.99%
- Borrow up to the total cost of attendance minus other financial aid
- In-school deferment and 6-month grace period after graduation
- Co-signer release available after 36 consecutive on-time payments

4.2 Student Loan Refinancing
- Consolidate federal and private student loans
- Fixed rates from 4.49%, variable rates from 4.89%
- Loan amounts: $10,000 to $500,000
- No application or origination fees

5. SMALL BUSINESS LOANS

5.1 SBA Loans
- Citizens Bank is an SBA Preferred Lender
- SBA 7(a) loans up to $5 million
- SBA 504 loans for real estate and equipment
- Competitive rates, longer terms, and lower down payments

5.2 Business Lines of Credit
- Revolving credit lines from $10,000 to $250,000
- Interest-only payments during draw period
- Annual review required
- Must provide business financials and tax returns

6. GENERAL LOAN POLICIES

6.1 Late Payment Policy
- Grace period: 15 days after due date
- Late fee: 5% of the past-due payment amount or $25, whichever is greater
- Three or more late payments in a 12-month period may trigger a rate review

6.2 Hardship Programs
- If you experience financial hardship, contact us at 1-800-340-5626
- Options include temporary payment reduction, forbearance, or loan modification
- Documentation of hardship required (job loss letter, medical bills, etc.)

6.3 Prepayment
- No prepayment penalties on personal loans, auto loans, or student loans
- HELOC may have an early termination fee if closed within the first 36 months ($500)
