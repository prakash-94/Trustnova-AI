Citizens Bank — Wealth Management & Financial Planning

1. CITIZENS PRIVATE CLIENT

1.1 Eligibility
- Combined deposits and investments of $250,000 or more
- Or mortgage relationship of $500,000 or more
- Dedicated Private Client advisor
- Priority service at branches and call centers

1.2 Benefits
- No monthly fees on checking, savings, or money market accounts
- Unlimited ATM fee rebates (domestic and international)
- No foreign transaction fees on debit card purchases
- Enhanced daily ATM withdrawal limit ($1,000)
- Complimentary safe deposit box (small size)
- Preferred pricing on loans and mortgages (0.125% - 0.25% rate discount)
- Annual financial planning review
- Priority scheduling for in-branch appointments
