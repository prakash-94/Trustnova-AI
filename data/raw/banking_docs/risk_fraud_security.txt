Citizens Bank — Risk Management, Fraud Prevention & Cybersecurity Policy

OVERVIEW

This document covers Citizens Bank's enterprise risk framework, fraud detection and prevention program, transaction monitoring rules, and cybersecurity controls.

ENTERPRISE RISK FRAMEWORK

1. RISK GOVERNANCE

1.1 Three Lines of Defense
- First Line: Business units own and manage risk daily
- Second Line: Risk Management and Compliance provide oversight, frameworks, and challenge
- Third Line: Internal Audit provides independent assurance

1.2 Risk Appetite
- Credit Risk: NPL ratio target below 3%; provision coverage above 100%
- Fraud Risk: fraud loss rate target below 0.08% of transaction volume
- Operational Risk: zero tolerance for regulatory violations; low tolerance for system outages
- Reputational Risk: zero tolerance for customer data breaches

1.3 Risk Committee
- Enterprise Risk Committee meets monthly; chaired by Chief Risk Officer
- Reports to Board Risk Committee quarterly
- Members: CRO, CCO, CFO, CISO, Head of Credit, Head of Operations

2. CREDIT RISK

2.1 Risk Rating Scale
- Score 80-100: Low risk — standard monitoring, no restrictions
- Score 60-79: Medium risk — quarterly review, standard controls
- Score 40-59: High risk — monthly review, enhanced monitoring, manager sign-off required
- Score 0-39: Critical risk — immediate escalation, EDD required, restricted services

2.2 Composite Risk Score Components
- Credit score: 35% weight (FICO model)
- AML risk rating: 25% weight
- Fraud history: 25% weight (incidents in last 24 months)
- Account age and tenure: 15% weight

2.3 Portfolio Concentration Limits
- Single borrower: maximum 15% of Tier 1 capital
- Industry sector: maximum 25% of total loan book in any single sector
- Geographic concentration: maximum 30% in any single state

3. FRAUD DETECTION

3.1 Machine Learning Fraud Scoring
- Real-time ML model scores every transaction 0.00 to 1.00
- Model features: transaction amount, merchant category, geolocation, device fingerprint, velocity, time of day, behavioral baseline
- Score thresholds:
  - 0.00-0.49: Low risk — auto-approved
  - 0.50-0.69: Moderate — step-up authentication required
  - 0.70-0.84: High risk — manual review queue
  - 0.85-1.00: Critical — auto-declined, fraud alert generated

3.2 Rule-Based Controls (supplemental to ML)
- Card not present (CNP) transactions > $2,000: additional authentication required
- International transactions from new devices: SMS OTP required
- ATM withdrawal > $500 outside home state: alert generated
- More than 5 declined transactions in 1 hour: temporary card freeze
- Address change + large transfer within 48 hours: manual review
- New payee + large ACH within 7 days of account access: hold applied

3.3 Fraud Categories Monitored
- Card fraud: counterfeit, lost/stolen, card-not-present
- Account takeover (ATO): unauthorized access, credential stuffing
- Identity theft: synthetic identity, first-party fraud
- ACH/wire fraud: unauthorized transfers, business email compromise (BEC)
- Check fraud: counterfeit, altered, check kiting
- Loan fraud: income misrepresentation, straw borrowers
- Zelle/P2P fraud: authorized push payment scams

3.4 Dispute and Chargeback Process
- Customer reports fraud via app, phone, or branch
- Provisional credit issued within 5 business days (Reg E)
- Investigation completed within 10 business days (45 days for international/POS)
- Permanent credit or reversal communicated in writing

4. TRANSACTION MONITORING RULES

4.1 High-Value Thresholds
- Domestic wire > $50,000: senior approval required
- International wire > $25,000: compliance review + OFAC screen
- ACH debit > $100,000 single item: next-day hold, manual release
- Cash deposit > $10,000: CTR generated automatically

4.2 Velocity Controls
- Max 10 external transfers per day per account
- Max $25,000 per day outbound ACH (retail)
- Max $100,000 per day outbound ACH (business premium)
- Zelle: $2,500 per transaction, $10,000 per day, $20,000 per month

4.3 Behavioral Anomaly Detection
- Deviation > 3 standard deviations from 90-day spending baseline triggers review
- First international transaction on account: SMS notification sent
- Transactions at 2am-5am local time on dormant accounts: alert generated

5. CYBERSECURITY

5.1 Access Control
- Role-based access control (RBAC) for all systems; least-privilege principle
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) mandatory for all employee systems
- Privileged access management (PAM) for admin accounts; session recording enabled
- Quarterly access reviews; terminated employees deprovisioned within 4 hours

5.2 Data Protection
- All customer data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3)
- PII tokenization for card numbers (PCI DSS compliance)
- Data loss prevention (DLP) tools monitor email, USB, and cloud uploads
- Customer data retention: 7 years; secure deletion thereafter

5.3 Incident Response
- P1 (critical breach): CISO and CEO notified within 1 hour; regulators within 72 hours
- P2 (significant incident): response team assembled within 4 hours
- P3 (minor incident): resolved within 5 business days
- Tabletop exercises conducted semi-annually; full incident response drill annually

5.4 Vendor Risk Management
- All third-party vendors with data access undergo annual security assessment
- SOC 2 Type II report required for critical vendors
- Penetration testing required annually for vendors with system integration
- Vendor contracts include data breach notification within 24 hours

6. OPERATIONAL RISK

6.1 Business Continuity
- Recovery Time Objective (RTO): core banking systems < 4 hours
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO): < 15 minutes data loss
- Backup data centers in geographically separate regions
- BCP tested semi-annually; DR test annually

6.2 Change Management
- All production changes require Change Advisory Board (CAB) approval
- Emergency changes require CTO + CISO approval; post-implementation review mandatory
- Release freeze: 2 weeks before and after major regulatory reporting dates

6.3 Model Risk
- All risk models validated by independent Model Validation team annually
- Model documentation (model card) required: purpose, data sources, limitations, performance metrics
- Champion/challenger framework for ML fraud models; monthly performance monitoring
