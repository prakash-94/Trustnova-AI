Citizens Bank — General FAQ

Q: What are Citizens Bank's operating hours?
A: Most Citizens Bank branches are open Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Hours may vary by location. Our online banking platform and mobile app are available 24/7.

Q: How do I open a new checking account?
A: You can open a checking account online at citizensbank.com, through our mobile app, or by visiting any branch. You will need a valid government-issued ID, your Social Security Number, and an initial deposit (minimum $25 for standard checking).

Q: What types of checking accounts does Citizens Bank offer?
A: We offer several checking account options:
- One Deposit Checking: No monthly fee with one deposit of any amount per statement period.
- Citizens Quest Checking: Premium account with higher interest and additional perks.
- Student Checking: Designed for students aged 17-24 with no monthly maintenance fees.
- Citizens Green Checking: Our most basic, eco-friendly paperless account.

Q: How do I reset my online banking password?
A: Visit citizensbank.com and click "Forgot Password" on the login page. You'll need to verify your identity using your SSN, date of birth, and email or phone number on file. You can also call our support line at 1-800-922-9999.

Q: What is Citizens Bank's routing number?
A: Citizens Bank has multiple routing numbers depending on your state. Common routing numbers include:
- Connecticut: 011101191
- Massachusetts: 011500120
- New York: 021313103
- Pennsylvania: 036076150
Please check your checkbook or contact your local branch for your specific routing number.

Q: Does Citizens Bank offer Zelle?
A: Yes, Citizens Bank customers can use Zelle to send and receive money directly through the Citizens Bank mobile app or online banking. Transfers between enrolled users typically arrive within minutes.

Q: How do I report a lost or stolen debit card?
A: Call us immediately at 1-800-922-9999 (available 24/7). You can also lock your card temporarily through the mobile app under Card Management while you search for it. A replacement card will be mailed within 5-7 business days, or you can request expedited delivery.

Q: What are Citizens Bank's overdraft policies?
A: Citizens Bank offers several overdraft options:
- Overdraft Protection: Link a savings account to cover overdrafts automatically ($12 transfer fee).
- Citizens Peace of Mind: If you overdraft by $5 or less, no fee is charged. If you overdraft by more, you have until 10 PM the next business day to bring your balance positive to avoid the $35 overdraft fee.
- You can opt out of overdraft coverage entirely, in which case transactions that would overdraft your account will simply be declined.

Q: Does Citizens Bank charge ATM fees?
A: Citizens Bank does not charge fees for using Citizens Bank ATMs. If you use a non-Citizens Bank ATM, a $3 fee per transaction may apply, in addition to any fee the ATM owner charges. Citizens Quest Checking customers receive unlimited ATM fee rebates nationwide.
