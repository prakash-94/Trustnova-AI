4. GENERAL CREDIT CARD POLICIES

4.1 Payments
- Minimum payment: Greater of $25 or 1% of balance plus interest and fees
- Payment due date: 25 days after statement close date
- Late payment fee: Up to $40
- Returned payment fee: Up to $40
- Payments received after 5:00 PM ET are credited the next business day

4.2 Credit Limits
- Initial credit limits determined by creditworthiness and income
- Credit limit increases may be requested after 6 months
- Automatic credit limit reviews conducted annually
- Over-limit transactions declined by default (opt-in available)

4.3 Dispute Resolution
- Disputes must be filed within 60 days of the statement date
- Temporary credit issued during investigation
- Investigation completed within 30-90 days
- Written notification of outcome provided
- File disputes online, by phone (1-800-684-2222), or by mail

- Virtual card numbers for online shopping
- Two-factor authentication for online account access

5.2 Travel Notifications
- Set travel plans through the mobile app or online banking
- Prevents legitimate purchases from being declined while traveling
- 24/7 global emergency assistance
