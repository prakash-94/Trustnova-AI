Citizens Bank — Wire Transfer & International Banking

1. DOMESTIC WIRE TRANSFERS

1.1 Outgoing Domestic Wires
- Available through online banking, in-branch, or by phone
- Cut-off time: 5:00 PM ET for same-day processing
- Funds typically received within hours (same business day)
- Fee: $30 per outgoing domestic wire
- Citizens Private Client: Fee waived

1.2 Incoming Domestic Wires
- No fee for receiving domestic wires
- Provide sender with: Citizens Bank routing number, your account number, your full name
- Routing number for wires: 011500120 (different from ACH routing number in some states)
- Funds credited same day if received before 6:00 PM ET

2. INTERNATIONAL WIRE TRANSFERS

2.1 Outgoing International Wires
- Available in-branch or by phone (not available online for most accounts)
- Fee: $45 per outgoing international wire
- Processing time: 1-5 business days depending on destination country
- Exchange rate applied at time of processing (bank rate + margin)
- SWIFT/BIC code required for recipient's bank
- Recipient's full name, account number, and bank address required
- Intermediary bank fees may apply ($15-$25)

2.2 Incoming International Wires
- Fee: $15 per incoming international wire
- Provide sender with:
  - Bank name: Citizens Bank, N.A.
  - SWIFT code: CTZIUS33
  - Routing number: 011500120
  - Your account number
  - Your full name and address
- Exchange rate conversion applied automatically for foreign currency wires
- Funds typically available within 1-2 business days

3. FOREIGN CURRENCY

3.1 Foreign Currency Exchange
- Available at select branch locations
- Common currencies available: EUR, GBP, CAD, JPY, MXN, CHF, AUD
- Exotic currencies may require 3-5 business days to order
- Exchange rate includes a spread (typically 2-3% above mid-market rate)
- Minimum transaction: equivalent of $50 USD

3.2 Foreign Currency Accounts
- Available for business customers
- Hold balances in major foreign currencies
- Useful for companies with international operations
- Interest may be paid on qualifying balances
- Contact Business Banking for details: 1-800-340-5626

4. INTERNATIONAL ACCOUNT ACCESS

4.1 Using Your Debit Card Abroad
- Notify Citizens Bank of travel plans before departure
- International transaction fee: 3% of transaction amount
- Citizens Quest Checking: No international transaction fees
- ATM withdrawals abroad: $5 fee per transaction (plus ATM owner's fee)
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit remains the same as domestic ($500 default)

4.2 Currency Conversion for Card Transactions
- Transactions in foreign currencies converted at Mastercard's exchange rate
- Conversion occurs on the date the transaction is processed (not the purchase date)
- Rate includes Citizens Bank's 3% international transaction fee (unless waived by account type)
