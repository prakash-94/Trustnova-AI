Citizens Bank — Corporate Overview & Regulatory Profile

OVERVIEW

Citizens Bank is a full-service commercial bank headquartered in the United States, providing retail banking, business banking, lending, wealth management, and treasury services to individuals, small businesses, and mid-market commercial clients.

CORPORATE PROFILE

1. COMPANY INFORMATION

1.1 Legal Entity
- Full legal name: Citizens Bank, National Association
- Charter type: National bank (chartered by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency)
- FDIC certificate number: on file with FDIC
- Federal Reserve member: Yes (member bank)
- Holding company: Citizens Financial Group

1.2 Mission & Values
- Mission: To help our customers, colleagues, and communities reach their potential
- Values: Integrity, Accountability, Collaboration, Excellence, Customer Focus
- Community Reinvestment Act (CRA) rating: Outstanding (most recent examination)

1.3 Scale & Footprint
- Total assets: approximately $222 billion
- Total deposits: approximately $175 billion
- Total loans: approximately $140 billion
- Branches: approximately 1,200 across the United States
- ATMs: approximately 3,300 nationwide
- Employees: approximately 18,000

2. REGULATORY STANDING

2.1 Primary Regulators
- Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC): primary federal regulator for national bank
- Federal Reserve Board (FRB): regulates holding company and systemic risk
- Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB): consumer protection supervision
- Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN): BSA/AML reporting and compliance
- Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC): sanctions compliance

2.2 Key Regulatory Requirements
- Capital adequacy: Basel III/IV framework; CET1 ratio target above 10%
- Stress testing: DFAST submission required annually
- Recovery and Resolution Planning: living will filed with regulators biennially
- Volcker Rule compliance: no proprietary trading; permitted fund activities only

2.3 Consumer Regulations
- Truth in Lending Act (TILA) / Regulation Z: loan disclosures
- Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA) / Regulation B: fair lending
- Home Mortgage Disclosure Act (HMDA) / Regulation C: mortgage reporting
- Electronic Fund Transfer Act / Regulation E: consumer electronic payments
- Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA): mortgage servicing
- Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA): customer data privacy

3.1 Retail Banking
- Checking accounts (personal, student, senior)

- Fiduciary trust services
