Citizens Bank — Mortgage Lending Guide

1. MORTGAGE PRODUCTS

1.1 Fixed-Rate Mortgages
- Available terms: 10, 15, 20, and 30 years
- Rate lock options: 45, 60, or 90 days
- Current rates (as of 2024):
  - 30-year fixed: 6.875% APR
  - 15-year fixed: 6.125% APR
- Minimum down payment: 3% (conventional), 3.5% (FHA)
- Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) required for down payments less than 20%
- PMI automatically removed when equity reaches 22%

1.2 Adjustable-Rate Mortgages (ARM)
- 5/1 ARM: Fixed for 5 years, then adjusts annually
- 7/1 ARM: Fixed for 7 years, then adjusts annually
- 10/1 ARM: Fixed for 10 years, then adjusts annually
- Rate caps: 2% per adjustment, 5% lifetime cap over initial rate
- Index: Secured Overnight Financing Rate (SOFR) + margin

1.3 Jumbo Mortgages
- For loan amounts exceeding conforming limits ($726,200 in most areas, $1,089,300 in high-cost areas)
- Minimum down payment: 10%
- Minimum credit score: 700
- Available in fixed and adjustable rate options
- Maximum debt-to-income ratio: 43%

2. FIRST-TIME HOMEBUYER PROGRAMS

2.1 Citizens Bank HomeReady® Mortgage
- Down payment as low as 3%
- Reduced PMI rates
- Income limits may apply based on census tract
- Homebuyer education course required (available online, free)
- Closing cost assistance may be available through state programs

2.2 FHA Loans
- Citizens Bank is an FHA-approved lender
- Down payment: 3.5% (credit score 580+), 10% (credit score 500-579)
- Upfront Mortgage Insurance Premium (UFMIP): 1.75% of loan amount
- Annual MIP: 0.55% of loan amount (for most borrowers)
- Gift funds allowed for entire down payment
- Seller concessions up to 6% of purchase price

2.3 VA Loans
- Available to eligible veterans, active-duty military, and surviving spouses
- No down payment required
- No PMI requirement
- VA funding fee: 1.25% - 3.3% (may be waived for disabled veterans)
- Competitive interest rates
- Citizens Bank is a VA-approved lender

3. REFINANCING

3.1 Rate-and-Term Refinance
- Replace existing mortgage with new terms or rate
- Available for primary residences, second homes, and investment properties
- Cash-out: Up to 80% loan-to-value (LTV)
- Appraisal required
- Break-even analysis provided to determine if refinancing makes financial sense

3.2 Streamline Refinance Programs
- FHA Streamline: Reduced documentation for existing FHA borrowers
- VA IRRRL: Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan for VA borrowers
- Both programs may not require a new appraisal

4. APPLICATION PROCESS

4.1 Pre-Qualification vs. Pre-Approval
- Pre-Qualification: Estimate based on self-reported financial information (not verified)
- Pre-Approval: Verified review of income, assets, and credit (valid for 90 days)
- Pre-approval strongly recommended before house hunting
- Pre-approval letter provided within 3 business days

4.2 Required Documentation
- Last 2 years of W-2s or 1099s
- Last 2 years of federal tax returns
- Last 30 days of pay stubs
- Last 2 months of bank and investment statements
- Government-issued photo ID
- Purchase agreement (once an offer is accepted)
- Gift letter (if applicable)

4.3 Timeline
- Application to closing: typically 30-45 days
- Appraisal: ordered within 3 days of application
- Underwriting: 7-14 days
- Clear to close: 3-5 days before closing date
- Closing: buyer signs documents, funds disbursed

5. CLOSING COSTS

5.1 Typical Closing Costs
Buyers should budget 2-5% of the loan amount for closing costs:
- Origination fee: 0.5% - 1% of loan amount
- Appraisal fee: $300 - $600
- Title search and insurance: $500 - $1,500
- Attorney fees: $500 - $1,500 (varies by state)
- Recording fees: $50 - $250
- Prepaid items: property taxes, homeowners insurance, per-diem interest

5.2 Closing Cost Assistance
- Citizens Bank offers lender credits in exchange for a slightly higher rate
- State-specific down payment and closing cost assistance programs available
- Seller concessions may cover some or all closing costs
