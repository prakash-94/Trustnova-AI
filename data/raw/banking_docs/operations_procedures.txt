Citizens Bank — Operations Procedures Manual

OVERVIEW

This manual defines standard operating procedures for branch and back-office operations including account opening, identity verification, transaction processing, wire transfers, dispute handling, and regulatory reporting workflows.

ACCOUNT OPERATIONS

1. ACCOUNT OPENING PROCEDURES

1.1 New Account Workflow
Step 1: Collect customer information (CIP minimum requirements)
Step 2: Scan and verify government-issued ID
Step 3: Run OFAC and fraud screening (automated, real-time)
Step 4: Complete risk rating questionnaire (AML risk assessment)
Step 5: Obtain required signatures and disclosures
Step 6: Fund account with opening deposit (minimum per product)
Step 7: Issue debit card and activate online banking enrollment

1.2 Required Disclosures at Account Opening
- Truth in Savings disclosure (Reg DD)
- Electronic Fund Transfer disclosure (Reg E)
- Privacy Notice (GLBA)
- Funds Availability Policy (Reg CC)
- Overdraft opt-in/opt-out election

1.3 Account Opening Holds
- New accounts: holds may be placed per Reg CC availability schedule
- Exception holds for new customers: up to 9 business days for first 30 days
- Large deposits (> $5,000): may hold amounts over $5,000 for up to 7 business days

2. IDENTITY VERIFICATION WORKFLOW

2.1 In-Branch Verification
- Primary ID: unexpired government photo ID (driver's license, state ID, passport)
- Secondary ID (if primary insufficient): credit/debit card, utility bill (within 60 days)
- Record ID number, issuing state, expiration date in core system
- Photocopy or scan ID and retain in customer file

2.2 Online/Digital Verification
- Document scan: AI-powered ID capture and liveness check
- Knowledge-based authentication (KBA): 4-of-5 questions correctly answered
- Device fingerprint and IP geolocation risk scoring
- Failed verification: account pended; customer contacted within 1 business day

2.3 Non-US Persons
- Passport required; ITIN or EIN for tax identification
- Additional documentation may be required depending on country of origin
- Enhanced screening for FATF high-risk country nationals

3. TRANSACTION PROCESSING

3.1 Funds Availability (Regulation CC)
- Cash deposits: next business day
- Local checks: 2nd business day
- Non-local checks: 5th business day
- Official/certified checks: next business day (first $5,525 same day)
- New accounts (< 30 days): holds extended per exception schedule

3.2 Cut-off Times
- Branch cash deposits: 5:00 PM local time
- ATM deposits: 9:00 PM local time
- ACH files: 5:00 PM ET for next-day settlement
- Domestic wire (outbound): 4:00 PM ET for same-day
- International wire (outbound): 2:00 PM ET for same-day

3.3 Stop Payment Orders
- Customer may stop payment on a check via app, phone, or branch
- Stop payment fee: $35 per item
- Effective for 6 months; renewable
- Cannot stop payment on certified or cashier's checks

4. WIRE TRANSFER PROCEDURES

4.1 Domestic Wire Processing
- Outbound wires require account holder authentication (MFA or in-person)
- Amounts > $10,000: verbal confirmation call to account holder required
- Amounts > $50,000: dual approval (teller + supervisor) required
- Same-day cutoff: 4:00 PM ET via Fedwire

4.2 International Wire Processing
- SWIFT required; intermediary bank and IBAN/sort code as applicable
- OFAC screening run before release; confirmed match = hold and escalate to BSA
- Amounts > $25,000: compliance review (up to 2 business days)
- Customer receives written disclosure per Remittance Transfer Rule (Reg E) for consumer wires

4.3 Wire Recall Procedures
- Recall request initiated via SWIFT gSRP or Fedwire recall message
- Recall does not guarantee recovery; beneficiary bank cooperation required
- Customer notified of recall attempt and outcome within 5 business days

5. OVERDRAFT PROCEDURES

5.1 Overdraft Coverage Options
- Standard overdraft service: covers checks, ACH, and recurring debits (opted-in accounts)
- Opt-out: debit card transactions declined at POS if insufficient funds
- Overdraft protection: linked savings account sweeps (no fee if available funds)
- Overdraft line of credit: pre-approved credit line; interest charged on balance

5.2 Overdraft Fees
- NSF/returned item fee: $35 per item
- Overdraft paid fee: $35 per item (waived if balance restored by end of business day)
- Maximum fees per day: 3 overdraft/NSF fees per account per day
- Accounts overdrawn > 60 days: account closed and referred to collections

6. DISPUTE AND ERROR RESOLUTION

6.1 Regulation E (Electronic Transactions)
- Unauthorized electronic transaction: must be reported within 60 days of statement
- Provisional credit: within 5 business days of claim (10 business days for new accounts)
- Investigation complete: within 10 business days (45 days for international or POS)
- If investigation not complete in 10 days: provisional credit becomes permanent pending outcome

6.2 Check Dispute (UCC Article 4)
- Alteration or forgery: customer has up to 1 year to report
- Bank's liability: recredit within 2 business days of discovery after investigation

6.3 Dispute Escalation
- Level 1: Branch/Phone banker (0-5 business days)
- Level 2: Operations dispute team (5-10 business days)
- Level 3: Compliance and legal review (complex cases)
- CFPB complaint: bank must respond within 15 calendar days

7. BRANCH OPERATIONS

7.1 Cash Management
- Teller cash limits: $15,000 per drawer
- Vault cash limits: branch-specific; set by Operations
- Dual custody required for vault access at all times
- Daily cash counts reconciled at open and close; discrepancies > $50 reported to Operations

7.2 Teller Transaction Limits
- Check cashing (non-account holder): $500 maximum
- Cash advances on credit card: $1,000 maximum per day
- Money orders: $1,000 maximum per item; unlimited per day

7.3 Opening and Closing Procedures
- Opening: dual custody vault open; system login audit trail; cash count verified
- Closing: all teller drawers reconciled; vault balanced; alarms set per security protocol
- Discrepancy reporting: submitted to Branch Operations before branch close

8. REGULATORY REPORTING

8.1 Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs)
- Auto-generated by core system for cash transactions > $10,000
- Teller must flag aggregated transactions by same customer on same day
- BSA team files CTR via FinCEN E-Filing within 15 days

8.2 Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs)
- BSA alert generated → Level 1 analyst review → BSA Officer approval → FinCEN filing
- 30-day clock from date of initial detection
- Tellers must report unusual customer behavior to BSA team immediately via SARs hotline

8.3 Gift Cards and Monetary Instruments
- Monetary instruments (money orders, cashier's checks) purchased with cash > $3,000:
  require customer identification and logging
- Gift cards: cannot be purchased with cash > $10,000 (structuring controls apply)
