Citizens Bank — Savings & Deposit Products

1. SAVINGS ACCOUNTS

1.1 Citizens Savings
- Minimum opening deposit: $0
- Monthly maintenance fee: $4.99 (waived with $200+ average daily balance or linked checking account)
- Annual Percentage Yield (APY): 0.01%
- Unlimited in-branch and ATM withdrawals
- Online/mobile transfers: 6 per statement cycle (Regulation D)

1.2 Citizens Quest Savings
- Minimum opening deposit: $0
- Monthly maintenance fee: $10 (waived with $25,000+ balance)
- APY: 0.03% (balances $0-$24,999), 0.04% (balances $25,000+)
- Relationship pricing available when combined with Quest Checking
- Complimentary financial planning consultation annually

1.3 One Deposit Savings
- Minimum opening deposit: $0
- Monthly maintenance fee: $5 (waived with at least one deposit per statement period)
- APY: 0.01%
- Great starter savings account for building habits

2. CERTIFICATES OF DEPOSIT (CDs)

2.1 Standard CDs
Available terms and current APY rates:
- 3 months: 4.50% APY
- 6 months: 4.75% APY
- 9 months: 4.85% APY
- 12 months: 4.90% APY
- 18 months: 4.50% APY
- 24 months: 4.25% APY
- 36 months: 4.00% APY
- 60 months: 3.75% APY

Minimum deposit: $1,000
Early withdrawal penalty: 90 days of interest (terms ≤12 months), 180 days of interest (terms >12 months)
Interest compounded daily, credited monthly
CD auto-renews at maturity unless customer opts out within 10-day grace period

2.2 No-Penalty CD
- 11-month term
- APY: 4.25%
- Minimum deposit: $5,000
- Full withdrawal allowed after first 7 days with no penalty
- Partial withdrawals not permitted
- Ideal for customers who want CD rates with savings account flexibility

2.3 Jumbo CDs
- Minimum deposit: $100,000
- Premium APY rates (0.10% to 0.25% higher than standard CDs)
- Available in all standard terms
- Relationship pricing for Private Banking clients

3. MONEY MARKET ACCOUNTS

3.1 Citizens Money Market
- Minimum opening deposit: $0
- Monthly maintenance fee: $12 (waived with $5,000+ average daily balance)
- Tiered APY:
  - $0 - $4,999: 0.01%
  - $5,000 - $24,999: 0.02%
  - $25,000 - $99,999: 0.03%
  - $100,000+: 0.04%
- Check-writing privileges (limited to 6 checks per month)
- Debit card access available

4. INDIVIDUAL RETIREMENT ACCOUNTS (IRAs)

4.1 Traditional IRA
- Tax-deductible contributions (subject to income limits)
- Tax-deferred growth
- Required minimum distributions starting at age 73
- Contribution limit: $6,500/year ($7,500 if age 50+) — 2024 limits
- Available as IRA Savings or IRA CD

4.2 Roth IRA
- After-tax contributions (no tax deduction)
- Tax-free qualified withdrawals
- No required minimum distributions during owner's lifetime
- Income limits apply for contributions
- Same annual contribution limits as Traditional IRA

5. SPECIAL PROGRAMS

5.1 Citizens Bank Education Savings (529 Plan)
- Tax-advantaged savings for education expenses
- Available for K-12 tuition (up to $10,000/year) and higher education
- Multiple investment options
- Gift tax exclusion: up to $17,000/year per beneficiary (2024)

5.2 Health Savings Account (HSA)
- Available to customers with high-deductible health plans (HDHP)
- Triple tax advantage: tax-deductible contributions, tax-free growth, tax-free qualified withdrawals
- 2024 contribution limits: $4,150 (individual), $8,300 (family)
- Debit card for qualified medical expenses
- Investment options available for balances over $1,000
