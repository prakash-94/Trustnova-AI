Citizens Bank — Complete Products & Services Guide

OVERVIEW

This guide covers all Citizens Bank deposit products, lending products, card products, digital services, and ancillary services available to retail and business customers.

DEPOSIT PRODUCTS

1. CHECKING ACCOUNTS

1.1 One Deposit Checking
- Monthly fee: $0 (no minimum balance required)
- Minimum opening deposit: $0
- Features: debit card, online/mobile banking, mobile check deposit, Zelle, bill pay
- Overdraft: standard overdraft service available (opt-in required for debit card)
- Best for: everyday banking with simple, no-fee structure

1.2 Clearly Better Checking
- Monthly fee: $5.99 (waived with $500+ monthly direct deposit)
- Minimum opening deposit: $25
- Features: all One Deposit features + 1 fee-free out-of-network ATM per month
- Interest: none
- Best for: customers with regular direct deposit

1.3 Platinum Checking
- Monthly fee: $25 (waived with $25,000 combined balance)
- Minimum opening deposit: $1,000
- Features: all Clearly Better features + unlimited out-of-network ATM fee refunds + free wire transfers (2/month) + dedicated banker
- Interest: tiered; contact branch for current rates
- Best for: high-balance customers wanting premium service

1.4 Student Checking
- Monthly fee: $0
- Minimum opening deposit: $0
- Eligibility: ages 17-25 enrolled in accredited college/university
- Features: standard debit card, Zelle, no minimum balance, 1 ATM fee refund per month
- Auto-converts to Clearly Better Checking at age 25

1.5 Joint Checking
- All checking products available as joint accounts
- Both account holders have equal ownership and access
- Requires both owners to sign account documents and provide ID

2. SAVINGS ACCOUNTS

2.1 One Deposit Savings
- Monthly fee: $0 (with any linked Citizens checking or 1 deposit per month)
- Minimum opening deposit: $0
- Interest: variable rate; compounded and credited monthly
- Transfers: up to 6 per month (Reg D applicable)

2.2 High Yield Savings
- Monthly fee: $4.99 (waived with $200 minimum daily balance)
- Minimum opening deposit: $200
- Interest: higher tiered APY; see current rate sheet for tiers
- Transfers: up to 6 per month

2.3 Money Market Account
- Minimum opening deposit: $2,500
- Monthly fee: $10 (waived with $2,500 minimum daily balance)
- Interest: tiered rates based on balance; higher rates than standard savings
- Check writing: limited check writing available
- Debit card: available

3. CERTIFICATES OF DEPOSIT (CDs)

3.1 Standard CD Terms and Features
- Terms available: 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24, 36, 48, 60 months
- Minimum deposit: $1,000 (standard); $500 (IRA CDs)
- Interest: fixed for term; compounded daily; credited monthly or at maturity
- Early withdrawal penalty: 90 days interest (terms ≤ 12 months); 180 days interest (terms > 12 months)
- Auto-renewal: renews at maturity for same term at current rate; 10-day grace period

3.2 Jumbo CD
- Minimum deposit: $100,000
- Premium rates above standard CD tiers
- Terms: same as standard (3-60 months)

3.3 No-Penalty CD
- Terms: 11 months
- Minimum deposit: $1,000
- Early withdrawal: allowed after first 7 days with no penalty (full principal + accrued interest)
- Rate: slightly below standard CD for same approximate term

3.4 IRA CD
- Available as Traditional IRA or Roth IRA
- Minimum deposit: $500
- Same terms as standard CDs
- Tax treatment per IRS rules for respective IRA type

4. PERSONAL LENDING PRODUCTS

4.1 Personal Loan (Unsecured)
- Loan amounts: $5,000 - $50,000
- Terms: 36, 48, or 60 months
- APR: 8.99% - 24.99% (based on creditworthiness)
- Minimum credit score: 660
- No origination fee; no prepayment penalty
- Funding: same-day to next business day after approval

4.2 Personal Line of Credit
- Credit limits: $5,000 - $25,000
- Variable APR: Prime + 5.99% to Prime + 14.99%
- Draw period: revolving (open-ended)
- Minimum monthly payment: 1% of balance or $25, whichever is greater
- Minimum credit score: 680

4.3 Auto Loan
- New vehicles: APR from 5.99% (terms 24-72 months)
- Used vehicles (≤ 5 years old): APR from 6.99% (terms 24-60 months)
- Maximum LTV: 110% of NADA/KBB value for new; 100% for used
- Minimum loan amount: $5,000
- No prepayment penalty

4.4 Home Equity Loan (HEL)
- Fixed rate; lump sum disbursement
- Amounts: $25,000 - $500,000
- Terms: 10, 15, or 20 years
- Maximum CLTV: 85%
- Closing costs: $500 - $1,500 (varies by state and loan amount)

4.5 Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC)
- Variable rate (Prime + margin); interest-only draw period
- Draw period: 10 years; repayment period: 20 years
- Amounts: $25,000 - $500,000
- Maximum CLTV: 85%
- Annual fee: $75 (waived first year)

5. CREDIT CARDS

5.1 Citizens Cash Back Card
- Rewards: 1.5% cash back on all purchases
- Annual fee: $0
- APR: 19.99% - 27.99% variable
- Sign-up bonus: $200 cash back after $1,000 spend in first 90 days
- No foreign transaction fee

5.2 Citizens Rewards Card
- Rewards: 3x points on dining and travel; 2x on groceries; 1x on all other
- Annual fee: $95 (waived first year)
- APR: 20.99% - 28.99% variable
- Sign-up bonus: 50,000 points after $3,000 spend in first 90 days
- Points value: approximately 1 cent per point for travel redemptions

5.3 Citizens Secured Card
- For customers building or rebuilding credit
- Security deposit: $300 - $5,000 (equals credit limit)
- Annual fee: $29
- APR: 24.99% fixed
- Reports to all 3 major credit bureaus monthly
- Upgrade path to unsecured card after 12 months of on-time payments

6. DIGITAL BANKING SERVICES

6.1 Mobile App Features
- Account balance and transaction history
- Mobile check deposit (limit: $5,000/day; $10,000/month for standard; higher for premium)
- Bill pay: up to 30-day advance scheduling
- Zelle P2P payments
- Debit card controls: freeze/unfreeze, set spending limits, travel notifications
- Loan payments and account transfers
- Document upload for loan applications
- Branch and ATM locator

6.2 Online Banking
- All mobile app features plus:
- Detailed account statements (7 years of history)
- Tax documents (1099-INT, 1098 for mortgage)
- Secure messaging with banker
- External account linking and transfers (limits apply)

6.3 Zelle Limits
- Per transaction: $2,500
- Daily limit: $10,000
- Monthly limit: $20,000
- Business accounts: $15,000 per transaction; $50,000 per month

7. BUSINESS PRODUCTS

7.1 Business Checking
- Basic Business Checking: $0 fee with 1 electronic deposit; 200 free transactions/month
- Business Advisor Checking: $22 fee (waived at $25,000 avg balance); 500 free transactions
- Business Premium Checking: $35 fee (waived at $50,000 avg balance); unlimited transactions; cash management included

7.2 Business Credit Cards
- Business Cash Back: 2% back on business purchases; $0 annual fee
- Business Rewards: 3x on travel/dining; 2x on office supplies; $95 annual fee
- Employee cards available; individual limits settable per card

7.3 Business Lending
- Business Line of Credit: $10,000 - $500,000; revolving
- Term Loans: $25,000 - $5,000,000; 1-10 year terms
- SBA 7(a): up to $5,000,000; 10-25 year terms (SBA preferred lender status)
- SBA 504: real estate and equipment; up to $5,000,000 SBA portion
- Commercial Real Estate: up to $25,000,000; 5-25 year amortization

8. ANCILLARY SERVICES

8.1 Safe Deposit Boxes
- Available at select branches
- Annual fees: $25 (3x5), $40 (5x5), $60 (3x10), $85 (5x10), $150 (10x10)
- Access: during branch hours; dual key required (customer + bank key)
- Contents not insured by FDIC; customers should obtain renter's insurance

8.2 Notary Services
- Available at all branches
- Free for Citizens Bank customers; $5/signature for non-customers
- Notary cannot notarize documents in which they have financial interest

8.3 Wire Transfer Services
- Domestic: $30 outbound; $15 inbound
- International: $45 outbound; $20 inbound
- Platinum Checking customers: 2 free domestic wires per month

8.4 Foreign Currency Exchange
- Available at select branches; 40+ currencies
- Exchange rates updated daily; competitive retail rates
- No fee for amounts > $1,000; $10 fee for amounts ≤ $1,000
- Advance order for less common currencies: 3-5 business days
