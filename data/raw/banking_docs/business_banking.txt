Citizens Bank — Business Banking Services

1. BUSINESS CHECKING ACCOUNTS

1.1 Clearly Better Business Checking®
- No monthly maintenance fee with $5,000 average monthly balance
- 200 free transactions per month ($0.50 per additional transaction)
- Free online and mobile banking with bill pay
- Free incoming domestic wires
- Mobile check deposit included
- Overdraft protection available

1.2 Business Advisor Checking
- For businesses with higher transaction volumes
- $22 monthly fee (waived with $25,000 average balance)
- 500 free transactions per month
- Earnings credit on collected balances
- Interest-bearing option available
- Dedicated business banker assigned

1.3 Business Money Market Account
- Tiered interest rates based on balance
- Check-writing and debit card access
- $12 monthly fee (waived with $10,000 balance)
- Great for businesses maintaining operating reserves

2. BUSINESS LENDING

2.1 Business Term Loans
- Amounts from $25,000 to $5 million
- Terms from 1 to 10 years
- Fixed or variable rate options
- Use for equipment, expansion, acquisitions, or working capital
- Collateral may be required for larger amounts

2.2 Business Lines of Credit
- Revolving credit from $10,000 to $250,000
- Draw funds as needed, pay interest only on what you use
- Annual renewal process
- Available as secured or unsecured
- Great for managing cash flow fluctuations and seasonal needs

2.3 Commercial Real Estate Loans
- Owner-occupied and investment properties
- Loan amounts from $250,000 to $25 million
- Terms: 5-year, 7-year, or 10-year with 25-year amortization
- Minimum down payment: 20%
- Interest rate options: fixed, variable, or hybrid
