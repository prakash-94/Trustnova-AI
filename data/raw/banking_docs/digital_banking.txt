Citizens Bank — Digital Banking & Mobile App Guide

1. ONLINE BANKING

1.1 Getting Started
- Enroll at citizensbank.com or through the mobile app
- Requirements: Active Citizens Bank account, email address, U.S. mobile phone number
- Two-factor authentication required for initial setup
- Username must be 6-20 characters (letters and numbers only)
- Password must be 8-32 characters with at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character

1.2 Online Banking Features
- View account balances and transaction history (up to 18 months)
- Transfer funds between Citizens Bank accounts (instant)
- Transfer to/from external accounts (1-3 business days)
- Pay bills through Bill Pay (one-time or recurring)
- Download statements (PDF) or export transactions (CSV)
- Set up account alerts (balance thresholds, large transactions, direct deposits)
- Manage beneficiaries and account nicknames
- Order checks and manage debit card settings

1.3 Bill Pay
- Pay virtually anyone in the U.S.
- Electronic payments: delivered in 1-2 business days
- Check payments: delivered in 3-5 business days
- Recurring payments: set up automatic payments on your schedule
- eBills: receive and pay bills directly within online banking
- Payment history retained for 18 months
- Rush payments available for a $9.95 fee (next business day delivery)

2. MOBILE APP

2.1 App Features
- Available for iOS (14.0+) and Android (8.0+)
- Biometric login (Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint)
- Mobile check deposit (limits: $5,000/day for standard accounts, $25,000/day for premium)
- Card management: lock/unlock cards, set spending limits, travel notifications
- Branch and ATM locator with real-time wait times
- Spending insights and budget tracking
- Push notifications for transactions and alerts
- Secure messaging with customer support

2.2 Mobile Check Deposit
- Endorsement required: Sign the back of the check and write "For Mobile Deposit at Citizens Bank"
- Photo requirements: clear, well-lit image of front and back
- Deposit limits vary by account type and tenure
- Funds availability: $200 next business day, remainder within 2 business days
- Checks must be in U.S. dollars drawn on a U.S. financial institution
- Retain the physical check for 14 days after deposit

3. ZELLE®

3.1 Sending Money
- Available through Citizens Bank mobile app and online banking
- Send money using the recipient's email address or U.S. mobile phone number
- Enrolled recipients receive funds within minutes
- Non-enrolled recipients have 14 days to register before the payment expires
- Daily sending limit: $2,500 (may vary by account type)
- No fees for Citizens Bank customers

3.2 Receiving Money
- Funds deposited directly into your Citizens Bank checking account
- No action needed if you're already enrolled
- You can request money from other Zelle users

3.3 Safety Tips
- Only send money to people you know and trust
- Zelle payments cannot be cancelled once the recipient has enrolled
- Citizens Bank will never ask you to send yourself money via Zelle
- Be cautious of scams involving overpayment or urgent requests

4. SECURITY

4.1 Multi-Factor Authentication
- Required for login from new devices
- Verification via text message, phone call, or authenticator app
- Security questions as backup verification method
- Trusted device management (revoke access anytime)

4.2 Account Security Best Practices
- Never share your password or security codes
- Use unique passwords for banking (not reused from other sites)
- Enable biometric login on your mobile device
- Review account activity regularly
- Set up transaction alerts for amounts over your typical spending
- Log out when using shared or public computers
- Keep your mobile app and device operating system updated

4.3 Reporting Fraud
- Call 1-800-922-9999 immediately if you suspect unauthorized access
- Report suspicious emails to phishing@citizensbank.com
- Disputed transactions investigated within 10 business days
- Provisional credit provided within 5 business days for eligible disputes
