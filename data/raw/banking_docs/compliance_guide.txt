Citizens Bank — Compliance & Regulatory Guide for Branch Staff

1. KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER (KYC)

1.1 Customer Identification Program (CIP)
All new account openings require verification of the following:
- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- Residential address (PO Box not acceptable as sole address)
- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, or state ID)
- Social Security Number or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN)

1.2 Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)
Enhanced due diligence is required for:
- Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) and their immediate family members
- Customers from OFAC-sanctioned countries
- Accounts with expected high transaction volumes (>$100,000/month)
- Non-resident alien accounts
- Private banking relationships with balances exceeding $1 million

1.3 Ongoing Monitoring
- Transaction monitoring systems flag unusual activity automatically
- Branch staff must report any suspicious behavior to the BSA/AML team
- Customer profiles must be reviewed and updated at least annually
- Changes in customer occupation, income, or address require profile updates

2. ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML)

2.1 Suspicious Activity Reporting (SAR)
A Suspicious Activity Report must be filed when:
- A transaction of $5,000 or more appears to involve funds from illegal activity
- A transaction is designed to evade reporting requirements (structuring)
- A transaction has no apparent business or lawful purpose
- The customer provides false or misleading identification

SAR filing must occur within 30 calendar days of detecting the suspicious activity. Do NOT inform the customer that a SAR has been or will be filed.

2.2 Currency Transaction Reporting (CTR)
- A CTR must be filed for any cash transaction exceeding $10,000 in a single business day
- Multiple transactions by the same customer that aggregate over $10,000 must also be reported
- CTRs must be filed within 15 calendar days of the transaction

2.3 Structuring
Structuring occurs when a customer breaks up transactions to avoid reporting thresholds. Examples:
- Depositing $9,500 on Monday and $9,500 on Tuesday
- Withdrawing exactly $9,900 repeatedly
- Using multiple branches to make deposits just under $10,000

If structuring is suspected, do NOT process the transaction. Report to your branch manager and the BSA team immediately.

3. FAIR LENDING

3.1 Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA)
Citizens Bank prohibits discrimination in lending based on:
- Race, color, or national origin
- Religion
- Sex, marital status, or gender identity
- Age (provided the applicant is of legal age)
- Receipt of public assistance
- Exercise of rights under the Consumer Credit Protection Act

3.2 Fair Housing Act
All mortgage and home equity lending decisions must comply with Fair Housing Act requirements. Redlining or steering customers based on the racial or ethnic composition of a neighborhood is strictly prohibited.

3.3 Community Reinvestment Act (CRA)
Citizens Bank is committed to meeting the credit needs of all communities in our assessment areas, including low- and moderate-income neighborhoods. Branch staff should actively promote products available to underserved communities.

4. PRIVACY AND DATA PROTECTION

4.1 Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA)
- Customer financial information must be protected at all times
- Privacy notices must be provided to all new customers and annually thereafter
- Customer information may NOT be shared with non-affiliated third parties without opt-out consent
- Physical documents containing customer data must be stored in locked cabinets
- Digital access to customer records requires role-based authentication

4.2 Data Breach Response
In the event of a suspected data breach:
1. Immediately notify the Information Security team (ext. 4500)
2. Document what data may have been compromised
3. Do not attempt to investigate independently
4. Preserve all evidence (do not delete emails, logs, etc.)
5. The bank will notify affected customers within 60 days as required by law

5. FRAUD PREVENTION

5.1 Internal Fraud
- Dual control is required for vault access and large cash transactions
- No employee may process transactions on their own account or accounts of family members
- All employees must complete annual fraud awareness training
- Whistleblower protections apply — report concerns to Ethics Hotline: 1-800-555-0199

5.2 External Fraud
Common fraud types to watch for:
- Check kiting: Exploiting float time between accounts
- Identity theft: Customer using stolen credentials
- Account takeover: Unauthorized access via social engineering
- Elder fraud: Targeting elderly customers for financial exploitation
- Romance scams: Third party directing customer to wire funds

Branch staff must verify identity for all wire transfers over $5,000 and all account changes (address, phone, email, beneficiaries).
